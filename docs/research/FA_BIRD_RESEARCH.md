## 1. Free-agent rights classes

Source: Master Guide § *Bird Rights* (Summary / Accruing / Bird Clock / Renouncing /
Trades-Waivers subsections), https://cbaguide.com/, read 2026-07-19.

CBA formal names (the table's vocabulary should map to these):
- Bird Rights (family) = **Veteran Free Agent Exception**
- Full Bird = **Qualifying Veteran Free Agent**
- Early Bird = **Early Qualifying Veteran Free Agent**
- Non-Bird = **Non-Qualifying Veteran Free Agent**

### Accrual (all classes)

To accrue any Bird rights the player must be (i) a Veteran Player, (ii) a Free
Agent, (iii) who **completed** a Player Contract *other than a 10-Day Contract*
(a Two-Way Contract can qualify) with the Prior Team. (§ Bird Rights → Accruing
Bird Rights.)

| Class | Prior seasons accrued | Accrual test (verbatim structure) |
|---|---|---|
| Non-Bird | 1 | satisfies the elements above but did not play more than 1 season with Prior Team |
| Early Bird | 2 | (i) played some/all of the two preceding seasons with Prior Team, (ii) changed teams by **trade or waiver claim** during the first of those two seasons, or (iii) signed with Prior Team during the first of the two seasons |
| Full Bird | 3+ | same structure over three preceding seasons |

**The Bird Clock (waivers):** the player must have completed his **most recent**
contract — i.e., not been waived under it. Waived during the most recent contract
→ clock **resets**; waived during a *prior* contract → rights **continue to
accrue**. (§ The "Bird Clock" – Completing a Contract, incl. the worked
three-year example ending in Full Bird.)

**Trades/waiver claims:** rights generally **transfer whole** to the acquiring
team (§ Trades/Waivers and Bird Rights; also § Effect on Bird Rights under
Trades: Siakam example — Toronto's Full Bird transferred to Indiana and was used
to re-sign him at 30% max). Exceptions:
- Waiver claim: to retain Full Bird the claim must have occurred in the **first**
  of the three counted seasons.
- **1-year-contract trade reset:** rights reset to Non-Bird if the contract (i) is
  not a Two-Way, (ii) is a 1-year contract (excluding option year), and (iii) the
  player would have Full or Early Bird after completing it. This is why such
  players carry an **Automatic (implicit) No-Trade Clause**. (§ Trades/Waivers and
  Bird Rights; § Effect on Bird Rights.)
- Expansion draft selection does **not** reset Bird rights. (§ Definitions area,
  "Bird Rights do not reset when Expansion Team selects a Player".)

**Renouncing:** renouncing relinquishes the ability to use the exception, but does
**not** reset the clock — re-signing via Room or Minimum Exception keeps accrual
going. A team may also **"renounce down"** from Early Bird to Non-Bird to escape
Early Bird's 2-year minimum length. (§ Renouncing Bird Rights; § Renouncing Cap
Holds → Effect on Bird Rights / Renouncing Down.)

### What each class lets the team sign (over the cap)

(§ Bird Rights → Summary table; dollar figure as printed 2026-07-19.)

| Class | Starting salary | Max length | Raises |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-Bird | greater of 120% of prior Salary/Bonuses, or 120% of Minimum Salary | 4 years | 5% |
| Early Bird | greater of 175% of prior Salary/Bonuses, or 105% of Average Salary ($15,235,500 for 2026-27) | 4 years; **minimum 2 years** (excluding option year) | 8% |
| Full Bird | up to his Maximum Salary | 5 years | 8% |

Maximum Salary tiers for reference (§ Maximum Salary): 25% of cap (1–6 YOS),
30% (7–9), 35% (10+), with Higher-Max ("supermax") criteria bumping a bracket.
"Salary" for the 120%/175% baselines is **Regular Salary** — the Guide lists
"Free Agent Cap Holds" and "Bird Rights Limits" among the uses of Regular Salary
(Salary minus signing bonuses and incentive compensation, § Regular Salary –
Uses).

**Declined rookie-scale team option:** if a team declines a rookie-scale TO, any
re-signing by that team is capped at the declined option amount (starting Regular
Salary, Likely and Unlikely bonuses limited to the declined TO). (§ Bird Rights →
Declined Rookie Scale Contract; also § Players Subject to RFA sidebar: the player
becomes a **UFA**, not an RFA.)

## 2. Cap holds

Source: Master Guide § *Cap Holds* (Purpose / Free Agent Cap Holds / Free Agent
Cap Hold Formulas / First Round Pick Cap Holds / Open Roster Spot Cap Hold /
Salary Cap Exceptions), https://cbaguide.com/, read 2026-07-19.

**When a hold exists:** a Free Agent Cap Hold is added to Team Salary for **any
player finishing the season on the team's roster**. It is based on **prior
contract type** and **Prior Salary**, where Prior Salary = last season's Regular
Salary + signing-bonus allocation + incentive compensation **actually earned**.

**Universal clamp:** a hold is never above the player's applicable **Maximum
Salary** nor below his applicable **Minimum Salary**; formulas exceeding either
bound are adjusted to the bound.

### The formula table (verbatim structure from the Guide)

EAPS = Estimated Average Player Salary = **104.5% of the prior year's Average
Player Salary**; Average Player Salary = total salaries ÷ 396 (30 teams × 13.2).
(§ Definitions.)

| Prior contract | Condition | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| Rookie Scale | coming off 4th year, prior salary **below** EAPS | **300%** of prior salary |
| Rookie Scale | coming off 4th year, prior salary **above** EAPS | **250%** of prior salary |
| Rookie Scale | coming off 2nd or 3rd year (declined option) | **Rookie Scale Amount only** |
| Restricted FA | — | **greatest of**: applicable UFA hold; QO amount; ROFR (matching) amount |
| Minimum-salary contract | — | new Minimum Salary amount, **never above the 2-Year-Veteran minimum** |
| Two-Way contract | — | Minimum Salary for a player with **0 YOS** |
| UFA (other) | Full Bird, prior salary below EAPS | **190%** |
| UFA (other) | Full Bird, prior salary above EAPS | **150%** |
| UFA (other) | Early Bird | **130%** |
| UFA (other) | Non-Bird | **120%** |

### First-round pick holds

**120% of the pick's Rookie Scale Amount**, effective immediately upon selection.
Removed when: he signs his NBA contract; the team loses/assigns his draft rights;
he signs a non-NBA contract (released the later of signing date or first day of
the regular season, **reinstated the following July 1** unless renounced); or the
team files the written no-sign statement (with the player's written renunciation
of a Required Tender) before the regular season — also reinstated the next
July 1. (§ First Round Pick Cap Holds.)

### Players coming off rookie-scale contracts

Covered by the table above: 4th-year completions hold at 300%/250% of prior
salary (EAPS split); a 2nd-/3rd-year exit (declined TO) holds at the Rookie
Scale Amount only. As RFAs, their operative hold is the RFA row — the greatest
of the UFA-formula hold, the QO amount, and the matching amount.

### Incomplete-roster charge ("Open Roster Spot Cap Hold")

Counted roster spots = players under Standard Contracts + players with FA cap
holds + draft-pick cap holds + outstanding offer sheets. Then: **12 − spots**;
if positive, multiply by the **0-YOS minimum salary** per open spot and add to
Team Salary. (§ Open Roster Spot Cap Hold — the Guide words step 1 as "take
total roster spots and subtract it from 12"; the worked intent is holds for
spots below 12.)

### Adjacent facts the table's consumers need

- **Exception cap holds:** when a team is under the cap by less than its
  available exceptions, the exception amounts themselves sit in Team Salary
  until used or renounced. (§ Cap Holds → Salary Cap Exceptions.)
- **Excluded from Team Salary:** renounced cap holds; Two-Way contracts;
  summer contracts; "1-Year Minimum Contracts"; Exhibit-10 bonuses; second-round
  pick exceptions until 7/30. (§ Team Salary – What's Excluded; see QUESTIONS on
  the 1-year-minimum line.)
- **Minimum-contract subsidy:** a 1-year/10-day/rest-of-season minimum contract
  carries a **cap hit at the 2-YOS minimum** (player still paid his YOS minimum;
  league benefits fund reimburses the difference). Nance example (2025-26):
  compensation $3,634,153 (10 YOS), cap hit $2,296,274 (2 YOS). The subsidy is
  **removed from Dead Salary** if the player is waived mid-season. A 2-year
  minimum (Minimum Exception) is *not* subsidized. (§ One-Year Minimum
  Contracts; § Minimum Exception vs. Minimum Salary Subsidy.)

## 3. Qualifying offers & RFA

Sources: Master Guide § *Restricted Free Agency* (The RFA Process / Players
Subject to RFA / Qualifying Offers / The Maximum Qualifying Offer / Terms for
Two-Way Contracts / Offer Sheets / ROFR Notice / Physical Exams), and § *RFA –
The "Arenas Provision"*; https://cbaguide.com/, read 2026-07-19.

### Who can be restricted

1. First-round picks finishing the **4th year** of a rookie-scale contract;
2. **Two-way players** who spent **15+ days on an NBA roster** during the regular
   season in the last season of the contract;
3. any other player with an expiring contract and **≤ 3 YOS**.

Clarification (Guide sidebar): a first-rounder whose 1st/2nd-year TO was declined
becomes a **UFA**, not an RFA, with the re-sign cap at the declined option amount.

### QO mechanics and timing

- QO deadline: **June 29, 5:00 PM** (can be made any time after the Finals). No
  timely QO → the player is a UFA.
- Player's acceptance deadline: **October 1**, extendable by the team any number
  of times up to **March 1**.
- Withdrawal: unilateral through **July 13**; after that only with player
  consent, and a post-7/13 withdrawal is **deemed a renunciation** of the team's
  FA rights (→ UFA, hold gone).
- QO required terms: 1 year, fully protected (skill/injury/illness), default
  payment schedule, physical-exam contingency permitted, all other terms
  unchanged. A signed QO carries an inherent no-trade (Cam Thomas 2025 example).

### QO amount (standard contracts) — the starter-criteria matrix

Starter Criteria met if: 4th season with 41+ starts or 2,000+ minutes, **or**
averaged across 3rd+4th seasons 41+ starts or 2,000+ minutes.

| Starter criteria | Draft slot | Salary basis for the QO |
|---|---|---|
| Met | 1–9 | own pick's scale salary |
| Met | 10–30 | 9th pick's scale salary |
| Met | 31–60 / undrafted* | 21st pick's scale salary |
| Not met | 1–14 | 15th pick's scale salary |
| Not met | 15–30 | own pick's scale salary |
| Not met | 31–60 / undrafted | greater of 135% of prior salary/performance bonuses, or current-season minimum + $200k |

\* second-rounders/undrafted need 2 or 3 YOS to use the 21st-pick basis.

Computation steps (Guide's four-step method): find the applicable **3rd-year**
scale salary per the matrix → apply that pick's 4th-year option percentage →
apply the raise (**+20% for picks 1–30**; none for 31–60/undrafted) → apply the
scale's QO percentage. Rookie-scale table lives at
https://cbaguide.com/resources/rookiescale/ (not re-read for this report).

**Maximum Qualifying Offer** (optional, offered alongside the QO, rookie-scale
finishers): max salary in Base Compensation, 8% raises, 5 seasons, fully
protected, no options/ETOs; withdrawable only together with the standard QO.

**Two-way QOs:** finished a 2-year two-way (or two consecutive two-way seasons,
same team) → QO is a **standard NBA minimum** contract with $102,000 protection
(2025-26, cap-indexed). All other two-ways → QO is another **two-way** at
maximum allowed protection; if no longer two-way eligible, treat as the former.

### Offer sheets & matching

- Offer-sheet window closes **March 1**; length ≥ 2 seasons (≥ 3 if a Maximum QO
  was made), option years excluded; cannot be for a two-way; the offering team
  must have **Room for the entire process**.
- Principal terms (what a match binds): length, payment arrangements, incentive
  compensation (team-performance bonuses deemed Likely for both teams), allowable
  amendments (protection, trade bonus…). Non-principal terms drop on a match.
- Match deadline: moratorium-received sheets — July 7 11:59 PM ET; otherwise 1–2
  days depending on receipt before/after noon ET. Matching can use Room, Bird
  rights, or the Minimum Exception. Matched → same-terms contract, no amendment
  for 1 year, no trade for 1 year without consent, never to the offering team
  that year, and **no sign-and-trade via ROFR**. Not matched → offering-team
  contract on the sheet's full terms, unamendable for 1 year.
- Relinquishing matching rights at any permissible time = **renunciation** →
  UFA.
- **Arenas provision** (RFAs with 1–2 YOS): year 1 of a sheet capped at the
  NTMLE, year 2 +5%, year 3 may jump to the year-3 max, year 4 +4.5%; offering
  team must hold Room equal to the **average** of the four salaries; cap
  accounting differs by outcome (offering team: average per season; matching
  prior team: actual per-season amounts, with an under-cap election to average).

### Hold behavior around the QO

Per the Cap Holds formula table: an RFA's hold is the **greatest of** the
UFA-formula hold, the **QO amount**, and the ROFR amount — so tendering a QO can
raise (never lower) the hold, and a matched-sheet amount can raise it further.
No QO by June 29 → player is a UFA → the plain UFA-formula hold applies (or no
rights if renounced). A team **cannot renounce an RFA directly** — it must
relinquish ROFR, which converts him to a UFA and automatically renounces the
hold. (§ Renouncing Cap Holds → Restricted Free Agent.)

## 4. State transitions (how a hold changes or disappears)

Sources: § Renouncing Cap Holds, § Cap Holds, § Triggering UFA Status,
§ Sign-and-Trade, § Retired Players Under Contract; read 2026-07-19.

| Event | Cap-sheet effect |
|---|---|
| Season ends with player on roster | FA hold appears at the § 2 formula amount (clamped to min/max) |
| Team renounces (written statement, effective ≥ July 1) | hold removed from Team Salary; **all Bird rights lost** (team signs him like any other team); clock not reset if re-signed via Room/Minimum |
| Player re-signs with team | contract salary **replaces** the hold |
| Player accepts the QO | 1-year QO contract replaces the hold |
| QO withdrawn after July 13 | deemed renunciation → UFA, hold gone |
| ROFR relinquished | automatic renunciation → UFA, hold gone |
| Offer sheet signed (offering team) | sheet amount ties up the offering team's Room for the whole process (§ Outstanding Offer Sheets counts toward roster spots) |
| Offer sheet matched | prior team: contract at principal terms replaces the hold |
| Offer sheet not matched | offering team: contract on sheet terms; prior team's hold/rights end with the process |
| Sign-and-trade | player signs with the **prior team using its Bird rights** (≥ 3 seasons, ≤ 4, year 1 fully guaranteed, before the regular season, no NTMLE/Room-MLE on the signing; acquiring team needs Room for salary + unlikely bonuses); salary then moves whole via trade (§ Sign-and-Trade) |
| Renounce-to-sign-an-offer-sheet, then the sheet is matched | the team may **"unrenounce"** within 2 days and restore the hold + rights, unless restoring would put it over the cap in the ways enumerated (§ Offer Sheets and "Unrenouncing") |
| Retirement (paid out) | not a hold: unearned protected compensation is allocated pro-rata over remaining cap years (§ Retired Players Under Contract) |

**Signing elsewhere:** the Guide's transitions above cover UFA conversion and
renunciation; it does not, in the sections read, print an explicit sentence
"the prior team's hold is removed when the player signs elsewhere" — see
QUESTIONS. (The mechanics imply it: rights/hold exist to re-sign the player;
first-round-pick holds have an explicit removal list, FA holds do not.)

## 5. Edge cases the table must represent

All read 2026-07-19.

1. **Two-way players** — contracts excluded from Team Salary entirely
   (§ Two-Ways and Team Salary); FA hold = 0-YOS minimum (§ hold table); can be
   RFAs via the 15-day rule; distinct QO forms (§ 3); a completed two-way
   **counts toward Bird accrual** (§ Accruing Bird Rights).
2. **10-day contracts** — a 10-day is excluded from Bird-qualifying completion
   ("completed a Player Contract other than a 10-Day Contract"); a 1-year/10-day/
   rest-of-season **minimum** carries the 2-YOS subsidy cap hit (§ One-Year
   Minimum Contracts).
3. **Exhibit 10 histories** — Exhibit-10 bonuses excluded from Team Salary;
   convertible to two-way before the regular season (bonus rescinded, conversion
   protection triggered); conversion protection/bonus band $5,000–$85,300
   (2025-26, cap-indexed). (§ Exhibit 10 Contract.)
4. **Rights traded** — Bird rights transfer whole with a trade or waiver claim
   (Siakam precedent), with the two § 1 exceptions (waiver-claim timing for Full
   Bird; the 1-year-contract reset that generates the automatic no-trade
   clause). A team re-acquiring context: after trading a player who is later
   waived, the sending team cannot re-sign him until the earlier of 1 year from
   the trade or the July 1 after his contract's last season (§ Resigning a
   Traded/Waived Player).
5. **Minimum-contract vets (the 2-year cap-hit rule)** — 1-year minimums hit the
   cap at the 2-YOS minimum regardless of YOS; hold afterward = "new minimum
   salary amount, never above the 2-Year-Veteran minimum". Two-year minimums are
   unsubsidized (Eubanks example). Waived mid-season → subsidy removed from dead
   salary.
6. **Option decline → FA transitions** (maps to our `v3_contract_options`
   codes) — Player option declined / contract completed → **UFA** on July 1
   (§ Triggering UFA Status). Rookie-scale **team option declined** → **UFA**
   (never RFA) with the prior team's offer capped at the declined option amount
   and the hold at the Rookie Scale Amount only. A 4th-year rookie-scale
   completion (option exercised and played out) → **RFA** if a QO is tendered.
   Waiver clearance and relinquished draft rights also produce UFAs.

## 6. Data-model implications (facts only — the minimal determining set)

From §§ 1–3, a player's **rights class** and **hold amount** are fully determined
by these facts (everything else is derivable):

**Rights class (Non/Early/Full Bird, or none):**
1. Consecutive "Bird seasons" with the current rights-holding team — which
   requires, per season: was he on the team, and how each stint ended
   (completed / waived / traded / claimed), because trades and claims transfer
   the count, a waive under the *most recent* contract resets it, and a waiver
   claim retains Full Bird only if claimed in the first counted season;
2. whether the most recent contract was **completed** (not waived) and was not a
   10-day;
3. the 1-year-trade reset predicate: last contract's length (1 year, option
   excluded), two-way or not, and whether he was traded under it;
4. renouncement events (current cap year), incl. renounce-down.

**Hold amount, given the class:**
5. **Prior contract type**: rookie-scale (and which year he's coming off),
   minimum (1-yr vs multi-yr), two-way, or standard;
6. **Prior Salary** = last season's Regular Salary + signing-bonus allocation +
   *earned* incentives (not just the cap hit — note the minimum-subsidy gap
   between compensation and cap hit);
7. prior salary **vs EAPS** (league constant per year: 104.5% × avg salary);
8. the player's applicable **Minimum and Maximum Salary** (needs YOS, and the
   max tier: 25/30/35% incl. higher-max criteria), for the clamp;
9. RFA-only: **QO amount** — needs draft year, draft slot (or
   second-round/undrafted status), the rookie-scale table for that draft year,
   and the **starter-criteria inputs** (games started and minutes in seasons 3
   and 4); plus whether a QO was actually tendered and any ROFR amount;
10. league constants per cap year: rookie scale table, minimum-salary scale
    (by YOS), EAPS, cap/max percentages.

**Status flags that change state, not amount:** QO tendered/withdrawn dates,
renounced flag, ROFR relinquished, offer-sheet outstanding, first-round-pick
hold events (signed / rights lost / non-NBA contract / written statement).

## QUESTIONS (cbaguide ambiguities for the operator/adjudication)

1. **Hold removal on signing elsewhere:** the FA Cap Hold section gives no
   explicit removal enumeration (unlike First Round Pick holds, which have one).
   The removal when a player signs with another team is implied by the rights
   framework but not stated in a sentence that can be cited. Needs confirmation
   before the table encodes it as a rule rather than a convention.
2. **"1-Year Minimum Contracts" in the Team Salary exclusion list** (§ Team
   Salary – What's Excluded) sits in tension with the subsidy rule that a 1-year
   minimum carries a 2-YOS cap hit (i.e., *is* in Team Salary at the subsidized
   amount). The exclusion most plausibly refers to the unsubsidized excess, but
   the Guide's list does not say so explicitly.
3. **Early Bird accrual wording** — the Guide's clause (ii) reads "had changed
   teams by Trade or Waiver Claim during the first two Seasons"; whether "first"
   means "first of the two counted seasons" (parallel to the Full Bird waiver
   rule) is implied by symmetry but compressed in the text.
4. **Open-roster-spot arithmetic** — the Guide words step 1 as "take total
   roster spots and subtract it from 12" then says a *negative* result means no
   hold; the operative reading (holds for spots below 12) is clear from step 3
   but the sign convention as written is self-contradictory.
5. **RFA hold before vs after QO tender:** the hold table's RFA row (greatest of
   three) presumably applies from July 1 while the QO can be tendered any time
   after the Finals up to June 29 — the Guide does not state which hold applies
   in the gap if the team delays the tender (moot in most offseasons, but the
   table's state machine will hit it).
6. **Two-way Bird accrual quality** — a completed two-way counts toward accrual,
   but the Guide does not spell out how two-way seasons interact with the
   Early/Full "played some or all of N preceding seasons" counting when mixed
   with standard-contract seasons.
